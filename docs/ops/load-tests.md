Generated by `pnpm load` (M1-104, ADR-082) on 2026-10-30 from `docs/ops/data/load-100k.json`; do not edit by hand.

Dataset: 100,000 tickets. Each tier ramps up over 30s and holds for 2m. Machine: Apple M3 Pro, 11 cores, 36 GB (darwin/arm64), server and PostgreSQL 16 on the same machine as k6. Every person rests 5 to 15 seconds between steps; a quarter of requests write.

## Sizing

From the 2,000-people tier: 5.2 ms of server CPU and 1.1 ms of PostgreSQL CPU per request, 0.085 requests a second per active person. Cores are sized for 60% use; add one API process per extra core.

| Active people | Requests a second | Taskmoor (API and worker) | PostgreSQL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 4.2 | 1 cores, 1 GB | 1 cores, 4 GB |
| 500 | 42.3 | 1 cores, 1 GB | 1 cores, 4 GB |
| 2,000 | 169.1 | 2 cores, 2 GB | 1 cores, 10 GB |
| 5,000 | 422.8 | 4 cores, 4 GB | 1 cores, 22 GB |

PostgreSQL memory follows the dataset: shared buffers of a quarter of memory, enough to hold the hot tickets and indexes. For 10M tickets plan 32 GB or more (deploy/README.md). Rerun `pnpm load` on the target hardware before committing to a size.
